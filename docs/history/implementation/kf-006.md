KF-006 added bounded immediate Fetch, durable group progress vectors, explicit monotonic
Commit, reset as an internal operation, independent group progress, and an apply-time
commit/rebalance barrier. Reads use immutable snapshots and separate positional channels.
Cancellation/deadline does not release actual read/write ownership; late success may publish
once and uncertain writes keep their barrier until recovery.
