## Limitations and handoff

File/directory flush and atomic-replacement semantics depend on the documented local
filesystem/device contract. Bootstrap without authoritative metadata fails closed. Standalone
KF-003 directories are not migrated. Failed or canceled requests can survive; retries may
duplicate. No producer deduplication, public Fetch/Commit, membership, retention or throughput
claim. Watchdog events require explicit orderly close and recovery; shutdown timeout leaves
the writer and lock owned until real storage completion.

KF-005 handoff: durable group definitions, leased membership and generation fencing. Carry
forward coordinator responsiveness, apply-time ownership validation, monotonic lease time,
unknown write outcomes and fencing around unresolved storage work. KF-005 remains unstarted.
