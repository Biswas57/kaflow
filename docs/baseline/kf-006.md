## Limits and handoff

Linux local persistent filesystems remain reference; file/device force, atomic replacement
and directory-sync ordering must be honored. macOS development/process-kill tests do not
prove physical power-loss durability. Tokens are fencing credentials, not authentication.
Commit does not prove processing; retries after unknown outcomes require current ownership.
Catalog V3 makes progress required and rejects downgrade. No retention/long polling/batching/
transactions/exactly-once. Inactive reset is internal only. Consumer admission excludes caller
values, transport buffers after handoff, OS cache and total JVM heap. Blocked readers/writer
keep real ownership; close may report incomplete cleanup rather than forcefully release locks.

KF-007 remains unstarted: asynchronous bounded waiters, lost-wakeup prevention, cancellation
and ownership revalidation without occupying a read worker while waiting.
